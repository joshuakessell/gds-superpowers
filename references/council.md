# Creative council

Use [guided interaction](interaction.md) for the user-facing exchange. At each unresolved design fork, synthesize two to four tailored choices from the actual proposals, with a custom-answer route. Use **Hook**, **Player decision**, **Solo cost**, **Risk**, and **Smallest test** consistently; preserve dissent without printing a fabricated debate transcript.

## Shared brief

Give every specialist the same approved theme, game contract, three design pillars, target player, scope exclusions, and current uncertainty. Do not share other initial proposals until each independent submission is recorded.

The six lenses are Combat Specialist, Build Alchemist, Explorer, Home-Base Builder, Risk Taker, and Time-Conscious Player. Read their individual briefs in `roles/` as needed. Treat these as analytical preferences, not autobiographies or actual gameplay experience.

## Procedure

1. Independent round: at most two substantial proposals per lens. Every proposal includes a simpler version and a falsifiable experiment.
2. Coordinator triage: group duplicates and shortlist at most three competing implementations of the design question. Preserve meaningful minority positions.
3. Cross-review: assign a contrasting lens. State the strongest argument for the proposal, then the strongest failure case. Producer evaluates total asset, code, testing, and maintenance cost. Art Director checks coherence.
4. One revision: the author can revise, narrow, or withdraw the proposal.
5. Decision: adopt, prototype, revise, defer, or reject. Record rationale, evidence confidence, cost, dissent, and conditions for reopening.

When the evidence cannot distinguish two plausible options, design a small comparison. Voting and averages are not proof of fun. Do not manufacture a debate transcript when there were no separate agent runs.

## Quality criteria

Evaluate player agency, feedback/readability, build interaction, replay variation, theme fit, accessibility, distinctiveness, and production cost. Use qualitative anchored descriptions such as `weak`, `promising`, and `observed in test` rather than fabricated precision such as a 9.3 fun score.

Assign a primary and secondary audience. An idea does not need to please every lens. A combat-focused game can include exploration through optional encounters without acquiring an open world; the base-building lens can influence a facility menu without demanding a city simulator.

## Feature admission

Each accepted feature must answer: What new decision appears? Which existing system does it interact with? When does the player first encounter it? What can go wrong? What is the minimum content needed? What evidence would cause us to cut it?

Choose one signature mechanic and at most three supporting systems for the first slice. Anything else enters a deferred list with a reopening condition. Avoid accepting one subsystem per agent simply to satisfy the council.
