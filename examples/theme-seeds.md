# Optional theme seeds

These are original illustrative directions from the earlier concept work. None is selected automatically, and all numbers and mechanics are proposals.

## Undertow: Salvage

Pilot a salvage craft through submerged districts. Temporary upgrades modify attraction fields, torpedoes, armor, drones, and electrical effects. Bank recovered material at risky deposit buoys; secure blueprints to build a barge workshop, rescue specialists, and open deeper routes. The signature hypothesis is that moving and concentrating loose salvage creates both combat opportunities and banking decisions.

A progression interaction could connect a magnetic run upgrade to a secured induction blueprint, a new workshop branch, later electricity-based offers, and a specialist who changes the starting craft. One underwater visual bible governs combat, choice cards, and the surface barge.

## Afterlight Express

Escort a ghost train through threatened stations. Temporary modules alter attack patterns, support cars, shields, and soul-rescue interactions. Secured passengers and station records restore the terminus, recruit crew, and unlock routes. The signature hypothesis is that a small train-car arrangement system changes combat coverage and rescue tradeoffs without requiring a large railway simulator.

A rescued engineer could unlock a starter-car family; successful use of that family could reveal a station objective that permanently opens a route. Run-specific cars still reset according to the approved contract. The home base does not preserve an entire winning run build by accident.

## Glasshouse Rebellion

A gardener fights through overgrown conservatories using living defenses. Temporary plant powers change damage, healing, rooting, and connections between summoned plants. Secured seed records and rescued specialists restore a greenhouse and expand future graft options. The signature hypothesis is that a constrained network of planted effects creates interesting positioning without turning the game into unrestricted base building.

An early seed unlock may enable a fungal relay during later runs; mastering a relay interaction could rescue a botanist and unlock a different starter seed. A single botanical visual language applies to every screen.
