# Illustrative build ladder

Proposed mechanics for demonstrating GDSuperpowers output. They are not balanced specifications.

**Magnet Crown:** pulling loose scrap also draws nearby small enemies slightly inward. The tradeoff is a denser threat near the player. Requires explicit pull caps and enemy exclusions.

**Induction Coil:** collecting scrap charges a limited electrical pulse. Its meter and discharge must be readable. A workshop unlock can add this power to future offer pools without granting it permanently every run.

**Torpedo Bloom:** a cooldown attack emits a small fan of seeking projectiles. Offer upgrades can alter spread or target selection, not just damage.

**Ballast Plating:** a temporary barrier rewards controlled positioning at the cost of a specified movement penalty. Test whether the tradeoff is understandable and fun.

**Conductor Wake:** after a successful dodge, the next induction pulse travels through a short wake. A combination requirement creates a build milestone rather than another permanent statistic.

The progression example separates permanent access to an upgrade family from receiving that upgrade in a specific run. It also preserves the distinction between banked material, committed discoveries, and powers lost on death.
